## Read this before you use it

Three properties are easy to misread, so they come first. The full account is in the data card.

1. **1,342 trajectories cover 1,003 distinct questions.** 339 questions have two trajectories each, from k=2 sampling. They are not duplicates: the highest pairwise Jaccard between a pair is 0.907, below the 0.92 deduplication threshold, median 0.681. Deduplicate on `meta.qid` for one per question.
2. **`meta.route` is the route the teacher *declared*, not the tools it *called*.** HYBRID is 1.4% of declared labels but 16.2% of enacted behaviour, because 196 trajectories declare VECTOR while invoking both vector and graph retrieval. Say which one your analysis uses.
3. **Route labels are weak by construction.** Inter-annotator kappa on the benchmark was 0.27 to 0.53, under the 0.65 target set before annotation. The GRAPH/HYBRID boundary is genuinely blurred. Treat labels as a reference signal.

Further documented characteristics: the teacher rarely declares HYBRID even when it behaves that way (10 of 212 cases), refusal-type questions almost all fail filtering (6 of 123 survive), seven records carry decomposed Unicode because the file ships exactly as trained, and both benchmarks are synthetic rather than user-written.

## Provenance

Trajectories were generated by **Qwen3-32B** (Apache-2.0) running a ReAct loop over three retrieval tools, then filtered through six tiers (schema, citation validity, quality judge, route resolution, near-duplicate removal, and a semantic tier), reducing 4,864 raw trajectories to 1,342. No proprietary model touched the training data, which is why this is redistributable. An independent audit put correctness at 40 to 45 percent before the semantic tier and 77.5 percent after.

## Leakage

Exact-match checks find zero overlap between the training set and every benchmark, and zero duplicate questions within each benchmark. An embedding audit in the paper reports a median nearest-neighbour cosine of 0.73 with one disclosed near-duplicate at 0.955, whose removal changes neither primary statistical test. Exact matching cannot detect paraphrase: re-run an embedding check before training.

## Not legal advice

Questions are synthetic, answers came from a language model, and no legal professional certified them. The citation metrics score existence, lexical support, and entailment. None of them certifies that a cited provision *governs* the question. Vietnamese law changes and parts of the underlying corpus will be superseded.
