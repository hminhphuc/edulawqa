# Data Card — LexRoute EduLawQA

Artifacts accompanying *Trajectory Distillation for Faithful Statutory Citations in Vietnamese Education Law QA* (KSE 2026).

This card documents what the data **is**, how it was **built**, and — in the Known characteristics and Limitations sections — everything we believe a reader could hold against it. We would rather state a weakness here than have it discovered later.

## 2. Provenance

**Source corpus.** Vietnamese education-law documents (laws, decrees, circulars) published by Vietnamese state bodies. Under Article 15 of the Vietnamese Intellectual Property Law, legal normative documents and their official translations are not subject to copyright protection. The corpus itself is **not** redistributed here; only questions, trajectories, and retrieved excerpts quoted inside trajectories.

**Trajectories.** Generated by Qwen3-32B (Apache-2.0) running a ReAct loop over three retrieval tools, then filtered. No proprietary model touched the training data — this was a design constraint, so the dataset is redistributable. Generation cost was roughly 5 USD.

**Training questions.** 1,989 written by Qwen3-32B from sampled corpus passages under per-route quotas, plus 209 generated from deterministic amendment templates: 2,198 in total.

**Benchmark questions.** EduLawQA-Route was frozen on 2026-07-03, *before* the teacher was selected, and was generated by an earlier pipeline from statute-anchored templates; EduLawQA-MultiSource is template-generated with no LLM in the loop. Neither was written by Qwen3-32B. All questions are human-curated for form, not for legal correctness.

**Route labels (in `meta.route`).** Derived from generation intent combined with an independent Qwen3-32B classification under `prompts/route_codebook_v2.md`, with teacher override where the trajectory disagreed. **No legal professional annotated these labels.**

## 3. Filtering pipeline

Raw trajectories (4,864) were reduced to 1,342 through six tiers. Tier yields:

| Tier | What it checks | Survivors |
|---|---|---|
| raw | k=2 sampling at temperature 0.7, plus a retry pass | 4,864 |
| F1 | valid JSON schema with identifier echo | 4,473 |
| F2 | citation validity: document present in evidence; (document, article) matches a digest; citation-context overlap ≥ 0.4 | 3,333 |
| F3 | quality judge (Qwen3-32B) ≥ 18/25, yield-gated fallback to 16 (fired) | 2,744 |
| F4 | route disagreement resolved in favour of the teacher (28% of cases) | 2,744 |
| F5 | near-duplicate removal at 0.92 Jaccard | 2,626 |
| F3.5 | semantic tier: per-sentence grounding at 0.6 plus teacher re-verification | **1,342** |

Filter code: `scripts/f35_filter.py`, `harness/cvrs.py`, `harness/f3_filter_judge.py`. Prompts: `prompts/`.

An independent audit (GPT-4o, n=80 per group) measured correctness at 40–45% for rejected and CVRS-accepted trajectories, rising to 77.5% after F3.5.

## 4. Known characteristics

These are real properties of the data. None of them is a bug, but all of them will be visible to anyone who looks, so we state them up front.

**4.1 — 1,342 trajectories cover 1,003 distinct questions.** 339 questions have two trajectories each (k=2 sampling), 664 have one. These pairs are *not* duplicates: the highest pairwise Jaccard similarity between two trajectories of the same question is 0.907, below the 0.92 deduplication threshold, and the median is 0.681. If you need one trajectory per question, deduplicate on `meta.qid`; note that for 10 of the 339 pairs the two trajectories declare *different* routes (`meta.route`), so keep the one whose route you want.

**4.2 — `meta.route` is the route the teacher DECLARED, not the tools it actually called.** These differ, and the gap matters:

| | VECTOR | GRAPH | HYBRID | fetch-only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Declared (`meta.route`) | 790 | 533 | 19 | — |
| Enacted (tools actually invoked) | 595 | 523 | 218 | 6 |

196 trajectories declare VECTOR but invoke both vector and graph retrieval. So HYBRID is 1.4% of declared labels but 16.2% of enacted behaviour. Any analysis of routing must state which of the two it uses. The paper's "teacher tendency of 16–36%" refers to *enacted* routes, not these labels.

**4.3 — Route label distribution is imbalanced and shifted by the teacher.** The silver pool of 2,198 training questions was labelled VECTOR 1,134 / HYBRID 543 / GRAPH 398 / REFUSE 123. After filtering, the surviving questions carry VECTOR 613 / GRAPH 375 / HYBRID 15. The collapse of HYBRID is not caused by the filters — 39.0% of HYBRID-labelled questions survive, comparable to VECTOR's 45.7% — but by tier F4: of 212 surviving HYBRID questions, the teacher declared VECTOR for 105 and GRAPH for 97, and kept HYBRID for only 10. The teacher rarely declares HYBRID even when it behaves that way.

**4.4 — REFUSE questions are nearly absent.** Only 6 of 123 refusal-type questions survive filtering (4.9%). Models trained on this data learn refusal behaviour weakly from the training set; the abstention behaviour reported in the paper emerges from grounding discipline, not from refusal examples.

**4.5 — Route-label agreement is low.** Pairwise Cohen's kappa between three cross-family LLM annotators on the benchmark was 0.27–0.53, below the 0.65 target set before annotation. The GRAPH/HYBRID boundary is genuinely blurred. We report this as a finding, not a defect to hide: benchmark route labels are a *reference* signal, and 140 unanimous questions form a high-confidence subset.

**4.6 — Seven records contain decomposed (NFD) Vietnamese characters.** Records at lines 34, 160, 418, 419, 432, 819 and 840 (1-indexed) are not NFC-normalised, a residue of the source crawl. We ship the file **exactly as it was used for training** so that results reproduce; normalising would make the released data differ from the trained-on data. Run `scripts/normalize_nfc.py` if your pipeline needs NFC.

**4.7 — One email address appears in the data.** `thongtinchinhphu@chinhphu.vn`, inside the digital-signature block of a government document, appears in one retrieved excerpt. It is the published contact address of the Vietnamese Government Portal, not personal data.

**4.8 — Three template questions lost their document number.** Records at lines 1105, 1127 and 1134 ask about "Nghị định /2026/..." and similar, where the template rendered an empty document number. A further 8 records (5 questions) carry a document number with a stray space inside it, the same template-rendering defect. Together they are 0.8% of the set and, like the NFD records, ship as they were trained on. `scripts/validate_release.py` reports both groups.

**4.9 — Category and difficulty are partly confounded in EduLawQA-Route.** The ten categories and three difficulty levels are *not* fully crossed: 11 of the 30 cells are empty. `scenario` is 100% hard, `definition` 100% easy, and `new_2025` has no hard items. A per-category difficulty effect therefore cannot be separated from a category effect. `scripts/validate_release.py --verbose` prints the full cross-tabulation.

**4.10 — The corpus is centred on education law but not confined to it.** Questions were generated from a 25,701-chunk corpus built around Vietnamese education law, which also contains cross-referenced documents from other ministries (labour, finance, police, defence, health). By the keyword heuristic in `scripts/validate_release.py`, 22% of trajectories carry no education-related term in question or answer; a few concern unrelated domains such as construction permits or detention rules. Of the 609 document numbers mentioned in training questions, 58% are Ministry of Education circulars, 19% government decrees, 11% labour-ministry circulars, and 5% finance-ministry circulars. Treat the set as education-law-centred, not education-law-only.

**4.11 — Digests are stored at the 1,200-character log cap; the teacher saw up to 2,500.** Tool-result digests in `user` turns are the version written to the log (cap 1,200 characters). The teacher that wrote each answer received the same digest capped at 2,500 characters, so an answer can cite a passage that the stored digest truncates. The students were trained on the stored 1,200-character version, so the file is faithful to training, not to teacher generation. Any analysis of "did the model see this evidence" must account for the gap.

**4.12 — Quoted statutory text carries source-crawl defects.** The corpus text was crawled and OCR-like errors survive in quoted excerpts; for example the phrase "Phật tiền" (a corruption of "Phạt tiền", monetary fine) appears 61 times. We do not correct quoted text, because the trajectories were trained on it as is.

**4.13 — Benchmark questions are synthetic.** EduLawQA-Route is LLM-generated from statute-anchored templates. EduLawQA-MultiSource is deterministically template-generated with no LLM in the loop, anchored in real amendment relations over 61 document pairs (95 distinct documents), with exactly two gold documents per question. Neither benchmark consists of questions asked by real users. The paper labels every MultiSource result a controlled probe rather than a general routing result.

## 5. Contamination and leakage

Checks below are re-derived from the released files by `scripts/validate_release.py`; the keyword-based domain estimate in 4.10 and the corpus-defect count in 4.12 are also printed by that script, while the filtering-tier yields in section 3 come from the research logs and cannot be recomputed from this release alone.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Exact duplicate records within the trajectory file | 0 |
| Distinct question IDs sharing identical question text | 0 |
| Exact question overlap, training set against EduLawQA-Route | 0 |
| Exact question overlap, training set against EduLawQA-MultiSource | 0 |
| Duplicate questions within each benchmark | 0 |

An embedding-based audit reported in the paper found a median nearest-neighbour cosine of 0.73 between training and evaluation questions, with one near-duplicate at 0.955 (question Q022). That item is disclosed in the paper and excluding it changes neither primary statistical test. Since exact-match checks cannot detect paraphrase, treat the zeros above as necessary but not sufficient, and re-run an embedding check if you train on this data.

## 6. Schema

Full field-by-field description in `docs/SCHEMA.md`. In brief:

- **Trajectories** — one JSON object per line, with `messages` (a chat transcript: one system turn, then alternating user and assistant turns; user turns carry the question and tool digests, assistant turns carry the route decision, structured thought fields, tool calls, and the final cited answer) and `meta` (`qid`, `route`). Turn counts run 5 to 11, corresponding to 1 to 4 tool steps. Median trajectory length is 2,784 characters; the file totals 4.5M characters. Every final answer names at least one document number in its text (6 records have an empty structured `citations` list); final answers mention 1.57 document numbers on average, and 981 distinct documents appear anywhere in the set, evidence digests included.
- **EduLawQA-Route** — `id`, `question`, `category` (10 values), `category_vi`, `difficulty` (easy 60 / medium 100 / hard 40), plus the route labels: `route_reference` (majority of three cross-family LLM annotators: vector 163, graph 17, hybrid 20), `route_unanimous` (true for the 140 questions all three agreed on) and `route_votes` (the three individual votes, so the disagreement is inspectable rather than hidden). No reference answers: scoring uses a rubric judge, not string matching.
- **EduLawQA-MultiSource** — `id`, `question`, `template`, `gold_docs` (exactly two), `anchor`, `category`, `difficulty`. The no-number variant adds `orig_question` and `variant`, and strips document numbers from 97 of the 120 questions.

## 7. Intended and non-intended use

**Intended.** Research on agentic retrieval, citation faithfulness, and distillation for Vietnamese legal QA; as a training set for tool-using students; as an evaluation benchmark under the released harness.

**Not intended.** This is **not legal advice and must not be deployed as such.** Questions are synthetic, answers were produced by a language model and validated only for citation existence and grounding, not certified by a lawyer. Citation metrics in the paper score existence, lexical support, and entailment — none of which certifies that a cited provision *governs* the question. Vietnamese law changes; parts of the corpus will be superseded.
